Second Credit

Free remakes of games that deserve another go.

The gameplay you remember from the 80s and 90s, rules and levels intact, rebuilt with 2026 looks: real 3D, water, light, particles and a synthesised soundtrack. Free and open source.

Now playing

Inspired by Boulder Dash (1984)

Glimmerdeep

Dig deep, grab the gems, mind the boulders. The cave rules are an exact port of GDash's engine, checked against 306 recorded replays, so every classic cave plays the way it always did.

  • The Descent: a campaign of eight caves of our own, each with its own light and a story between them
  • Plays BDCFF cave files: bring your own packs
  • Crystal gems, falling boulders, fireflies and butterflies in 3D
  • Attract mode, death and win animations, music and effects

Inspired by Rampart (1990)

Bastion Coast

Build walls, aim cannons, hold the coast. Place cannons, fight off the fleet, then patch your walls with falling pieces before the timer runs out.

  • The Coastline: five islands to hold one after the other, with a story between them
  • Local versus for 2 or 3 players (keyboard, mouse and gamepads): shell each other's walls, last castle standing wins
  • Live territory and a smart hole finder that points at the gaps
  • Animated sea with swell, foam and reflections; sinking ships
  • Friendly fire on your own walls, like the original

Inspired by Fruity Frank (1984)

Fruitburrow

Dig the garden, pick the fruit, drop the apples. Tunnel through a slice of soil while plum monsters chase you down your own passages; undermine an apple to squash them, or send your ball bouncing along the tunnels.

  • The collection's first rigged, animated character: a gardener who walks, digs, throws and cheers
  • Soil you carve block by block, with stones and roots in the cross-section
  • Monsters that give up on the tunnels and start digging; day turns to dusk garden after garden

Inspired by Alley Cat (1984)

Whisker Alley

A moonlit alley, a ginger tomcat and nine lives. Bounce off the trash-can lids onto the fence, ride the washing lines and leap through whichever window swings open, while the bulldog prowls below and the neighbours throw their boots.

  • Five rooms behind the windows: the fishbowl, the big cheese, the bird cage, the dogs' dinner
  • Every third room, a serenade: climb a tower of drifting hearts to the lady cat
  • Rigged cats and bulldogs, a jazzy late-night swing, warm light spilling from every window

Inspired by Winter Games (1985)

Frostpeak Games

A snowy valley, a flame on the plaza and up to four players taking turns. Stride in rhythm round the ice oval, then tuck, spring and hold your line off the K90 hill, and climb the podium while your flag goes up.

  • Speed skating 500 m against a rival, with a stride meter that rewards rhythm
  • Ski jump: in-run tuck, take-off timing, flight balance, telemark landing, five judges
  • Invented nations, a medal ceremony and a medal table; snow that sparkles, ice that reflects

Inspired by Cannon Fodder (1993)

Muddy Boots

Four rookies, a jungle and a long way home. Click to march the squad in single file, hold the button to open fire, and bring the huts down with grenades before they empty onto you.

  • Squad orders with path finding, grenades that arc, rockets that smoke, rivers to swim
  • Huts, hostages to walk back to the tent, hidden mines, recruits carried from mission to mission
  • Two campaigns with a story between missions: First Tour and The Long Monsoon
  • Reads the original game's map files from your own copy, locally

Inspired by Double Dragon (1987)

Neon Knuckles

Two brothers, one wet night and a gang that owns the street. Punch your way along Neon Row, down to the docks and up onto the rooftops, alone or with a friend on the same keyboard.

  • Jabs, hooks, jump kicks, knees and elbows; grab a dazed thug and throw them into their friends
  • Bats, knives and crates to pick up; thugs, knifers, bruisers and a boss with his own moves
  • Hit-stop, screen shake and a synthwave score over neon, rain-slick asphalt and a city skyline

Inspired by Z (1996)

Iron Flags

No base building, no mining: plant a robot on a flag and every factory, radar and gun in that territory changes sides. The more ground you hold, the faster you build. Then flatten the enemy fort.

  • Grunts, psychos, snipers, toughs, pyros and lasers; jeeps, three tanks, APCs and missile launchers
  • A computer general that expands, defends its fort and pushes when its army is ready
  • Desert, ice, jungle, city and volcanic worlds; reads Zod Engine maps from your own files, locally

Inspired by Bomberman (1983)

Blastyard

Four bombers, one yard, no hard feelings. Drop bombs, chain the blasts, grab the power-ups, and be the last one standing when the arena starts closing in.

  • Up to four players on one keyboard and gamepads; computer bombers fill the empty seats
  • Chains, bomb kicks, a cursed skull, sudden death; garden, factory and ice arenas
  • Solo stages with blobs, bats, ghosts and stompers, and an exit hidden under a crate

Inspired by Lode Runner (1983)

Ingot Run

Grab the gold, dig the floor, keep running. A lantern-lit temple mine of bricks, ladders and ropes, and guards who know every shortcut. Blast a hole, let them fall in, and run over their heads.

  • Holes that refill with whoever is inside; gold that guards pick up and drop
  • Five levels of our own, and it reads the plain-text level sets of the free remakes
  • Rigged runner and guards on the new animation system

Inspired by Sokoban (1982)

Crate Keeper

Push, never pull. A lamp-lit harbour warehouse at dusk, crates that slide and glow once they sit on their marks, and all the time you need to think.

  • Undo and redo all the way back, click to walk, stars for matching par
  • Eight puzzles of our own; loads the standard Sokoban collections you have
  • A calm harbour theme, gulls and waves in the background

How it's made

Built with an AI pair

Claude Code writes most of the code, models and sound; the owner steers, plays and judges. Every step is a small, runnable commit, and the whole build is recorded as a construction movie.

Open tools only

Godot 4 for the games, Blender scripts for every 3D model, NumPy and FluidSynth for the sound and music. CC0 textures and fonts. No paid services.

Your levels still work

If the original had maps, we read them: BDCFF caves today, and more formats to come. The files stay on your machine; none of the original data ships with the game.

New art, same soul

No original names, sprites, sounds or code. Just the rules and the feel of a game worth a second credit, with a fresh coat of paint.
