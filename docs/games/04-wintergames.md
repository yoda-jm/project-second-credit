# 4. Winter Games (1985)

*Original: Epyx · C64 / Apple II · Sports / Party. Remake effort: medium. Free-version gap: **wide open**.*

The final title must be an original name (see [../legal.md](../legal.md)).

## Core loop

A set of winter sports events (ski jump, biathlon, speed skating, figure skating, hot dog aerials, bobsled), each with its own joystick timing or rhythm control, played in turn by up to 8 players.

## Gameplay

Players pick a country (complete with anthem) and compete in turn across winter sports events. Each event has its own control scheme: waggling or rhythmic left-right joystick movement for speed skating and biathlon skiing, precise timing for the ski jump take-off and landing, and chained moves for figure skating and hot dog aerials. Medals and points are tallied across events.

## Levels

Seven events on the C64 version, opened by a ceremony. You can practise a single event, compete in one, or play them all in sequence as a full games.

## The challenge

Each event is a separate motor-skill test, from stamina-heavy waggling to split-second timing and memorised routines. Mastery comes from practising the exact rhythm of each control.

## What makes it unique

The fun came from how the controls were built. Each event turned the joystick into a physical performance, and the multiplayer hot-seat made it a living-room party game long before that was a genre.

## Remake angle

The controls were the fun, so a remake should lean on today's inputs: triggers, gyro aiming, rumble and online play in turn. Snow, ice and alpine lighting are easy wins for modern rendering.

Motion controls, trigger pressure and rumble for each event. Snow and ice shaders, alpine lighting, crowd atmosphere, and online or couch tournaments with national anthems.

## Map compatibility

None: the events are fixed content.
