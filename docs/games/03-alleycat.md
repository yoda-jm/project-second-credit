# 3. Alley Cat (1984)

*Original: Synapse Software (Bill Williams) · IBM PC · Action / Platform. Remake effort: small. Free-version gap: **wide open**.*

The final title must be an original name (see [../legal.md](../legal.md)).

## Core loop

A stray cat leaps between fences, trash cans and clotheslines, jumps through open windows into mini-rooms (fishbowl, mice in cheese, sleeping dogs) to win the heart of a lady cat.

## Gameplay

The player controls Freddy, an alley cat, running and jumping across trash cans, the backyard fence and swaying clotheslines to leap into the open windows of an apartment building. Each window leads to a room with a mini-challenge, such as catching mice darting out of a giant cheese, eating fish from a fishbowl while avoiding electric eels, drinking milk from bowls next to sleeping dogs, or grabbing a bird from its cage. Hazards include the bulldog in the alley, objects thrown from windows by irritated residents, and a broom that chases Freddy out of rooms. Completing a room earns points and a chance to court Felicia, the female cat, by climbing a stack of hearts while dodging cupid's arrows.

## Levels

The game loops between the alley hub and a set of distinct room mini-games chosen by which window Freddy enters. After reaching Felicia the cycle restarts at a higher difficulty and speed.

## The challenge

Timing jumps onto moving clotheslines and open windows is tricky, and each room has its own rules and a short time limit. Players typically fail by being caught by the dog, being knocked down by thrown objects, or running out of time inside a room.

## What makes it unique

Alley Cat was one of the most charming and memorable early IBM PC games, squeezing lively animation and humor out of CGA graphics and the PC speaker. Its combination of a platforming hub with varied micro-games anticipated later mini-game collections.

## Remake angle

Great charm potential: a moonlit city alley with warm window light, furry cat animation, and each window room a tiny handcrafted diorama. Short, well-bounded mini-games.

The alley and apartments could become a lively, moonlit cartoon neighborhood with expressive animal animation, physics-driven thrown objects and interactive clutter in each room. Each mini-game room could get its own visual style and lighting while keeping the original rules and timing.

## Map compatibility

No maps: fixed alley and window rooms. rhuizer/alleycat (GPL-3.0) is a reimplementation of the IBM PC version to use as a behaviour reference.

## Existing free versions

Only hobby remakes exist; the best (Remeow, 2018) is enhanced-2D closed freeware. No open, modern-premium Alley Cat exists and there's no commercial remake either.
