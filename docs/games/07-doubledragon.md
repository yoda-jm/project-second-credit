# 7. Double Dragon (1987)

*Original: Technōs Japan · Arcade · Beat 'em up. Remake effort: medium. Free-version gap: **partly covered**.*

The final title must be an original name (see [../legal.md](../legal.md)).

## Core loop

Billy and Jimmy Lee fight through a city gang to rescue Marian, with punches, kicks, throws and weapons picked up from enemies.

## Gameplay

You walk through side-scrolling stages on a depth plane, fighting gang members with punches, kicks, jump kicks, an elbow strike, and grabs that lead into knee strikes and throws. Enemies drop weapons such as bats, whips, knives and dynamite, and you can also throw boxes, oil drums and rocks. Two players fight together.

## Levels

Four missions: city streets, a factory, a forest and the gang's hideout, each ending with a boss fight. In two-player mode the brothers fight each other at the end to decide who wins Marian.

## The challenge

Enemies surround you and hit hard, and big bruisers like Abobo can take off a lot of health quickly. Crowd control and spacing matter, and some platforms and pits can kill you instantly. Players famously discovered that the elbow strike trivialised many fights.

## What makes it unique

It defined the co-op beat 'em up. Weapon pickups, grabs and throws, and the bitter versus ending between the two brothers were all firsts or near-firsts that the whole genre copied.

## Remake angle

Neon-soaked 80s streets, rain, weapon physics and meaty hit-stop. Two-player co-op is the draw. The combat has to feel heavy, which is animation work more than rules work.

Heavy hit-stop, ragdoll knockdowns, rain-slick neon streets and dynamic crowds. Weapons with real physics and online co-op.

## Map compatibility

None worth targeting: OpenBOR fan mods contain the original IP.

## Existing free versions

Many free fan remakes exist (OpenBOR, Neo Geo homebrew) but they're IP-infringing sprite games, not open content, and not premium-modern; the modern-look slot is taken by recent commercial entries, so a free original beat-'em-up in the DD spirit would face strong comparison.
